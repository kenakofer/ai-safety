AI Safety Evidence Dashboard

Where the evidence on three contested questions disagrees, and why. Each dot is a paper, placed at the answer its authors would give; the small dots around it are outside critiques that change how it bears on the question.

Height = how the paper's authors would answer the question, estimated by a language model from their own text. A dark ring means an author has confirmed or corrected it; their answer replaces the estimate.

Tap or click a dot to see the paper's details.

Height = authors' answer (−2 clearly not … +2 clearly yes); dot size = how relevant the authors would say their work is; colour = dominant model family; satellites = outside critiques kept by a relevance filter, with verbatim quotes. A model's own judgment of each paper's evidence is in the detail panel, collapsed. Data: papers.json · discussion.json · questions.json.
