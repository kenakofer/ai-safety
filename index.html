AI Safety Evidence Dashboard

Where the evidence on three contested questions disagrees, and why. Each dot is a paper, grouped by what it actually measured; the small dots around it are outside critiques that change how it bears on the question.

This view plots a language model's judgment of each paper's stance. Treat it as one reader's opinion: the scores share one model family's priors.

Tap or click a dot to see the paper's details.

By method: lanes = what each paper studied; colour = dominant model family; satellites = outside critiques kept by a relevance filter, with verbatim quotes. LLM judgment: −2 strongly against … +2 strongly for; dot size = evidence strength, opacity = judge confidence. Data: papers.json · discussion.json · questions.json.
